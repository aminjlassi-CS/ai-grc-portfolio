# Brightwater Credit Union (Fictional)
> This is a fictional organization created for portfolio purposes.

## Overview
New York state-chartered, federally insured credit union.
Size, location in New York, members, services.

## Regulatory Environment
- GLBA, implemented through NCUA Part 748
- NYDFS 23 NYCRR Part 500 (Cybersecurity Requirements)
- New York SHIELD Act (breach notification)
- NYDFS industry letter on AI cybersecurity risks (October 2024)
- Framework adopted: NIST CSF 2.0

## AI Systems in Scope
1. "Ask Brightwater" – member-facing chatbot
   - LLM provider: Nimbus AI (fictional)
   - RAG over member FAQs and policy documents
   - Available on website and mobile app
2. Staff use of generative AI tools (drafting, research)

## Out of Scope
Core banking systems, payment processing.

## Key Stakeholders
CEO, CISO, CIO, Chief Compliance Officer, Head of Member Services.

## Assessment Objective
Identify and treat AI risks before the chatbot launches, aligned with NIST AI RMF.
